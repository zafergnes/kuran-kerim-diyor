## English

- Name: **Quran Says: Read & Reflect**
- App Store subtitle: **Read, Listen & Reflect**
- Play short description: **Read, listen and explore verses in their nearby context.**
- Keywords: `quran,translation,verse,audio,reflection,reading`
- Description: **Quran Says offers a calm way to read, listen and reflect. Follow your reading progress, save favorites, build collections, choose a reciter and use the experience in six languages. A small AI button lets you ask questions limited to the selected verse and its nearby Quran context. AI responses support education and reflection; they are not translations, fatwas or religious authority. Core reading and verse discussion work without an account.
